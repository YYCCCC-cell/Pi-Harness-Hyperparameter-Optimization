## What the assignment does not prescribe

The assignment names four harness candidates and three benchmark candidates; it does not preselect Pi or SkillsBench. It requires at least one developed method, not three implementations. It does not mandate Interaction-Aware HPO, a particular model/provider, a number of search seeds, a particular validation percentage, a specific dollar budget, a multi-fidelity scheduler, or fANOVA specifically. Its sensitivity requirement permits an appropriate ablation method. The five-day horizon is the user's constraint; no submission date, marking weights, or team size is stated in this file. P017–P023（本地或历史材料，未打包：`sources/research/assignment_extracted.txt#L17`）, P033–P034（本地或历史材料，未打包：`sources/research/assignment_extracted.txt#L33`）, P055（本地或历史材料，未打包：`sources/research/assignment_extracted.txt#L55`）.

The course text's examples of context limits and step budgets are general examples across harnesses. They do not establish that Pi exposes a setting with either name. Each selected Pi parameter needs its own official schema/documentation evidence.
